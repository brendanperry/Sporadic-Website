75 Soft challenge

75 Soft is a 75 day challenge built around a handful of daily habits, and it's an unofficial, easier take on 75 Hard. The rules vary by source, but a common version is: eat mostly healthy, exercise 30+ minutes 4 times a week, drink 3 liters of water, read 10 pages, meditate 5 minutes, and take a photo on day 1 and day 75 [1]. If you miss a day, you don't start over. You just pick back up the next day [2].

What are the 75 Soft rules?

There's no official version. 75 Soft grew out of social media, so the rules change depending on where you look [1]. Here are the two versions you'll see most often.

Both lists come from the articles linked below [1] [2]. The best part of either one is that missing a day doesn't end it. WebMD puts it plainly: you aren't punished if you miss one of the daily goals [2].

Pick your version before day 1

Since there's no official rulebook, the first thing to do is decide exactly what your 75 Soft looks like and write it down. If you leave it fuzzy, it's really easy to keep shrinking the rules on the days you're tired.

Here's what I'd nail down. The first is how much and how often you'll exercise, and what counts. WebMD says walking and yoga count, and that a weekly rest day is part of the plan [2]. The second is what "eating well" means for you, in a way you can answer yes or no at the end of the day.

Before you start

75 Soft is a gentle challenge, but it still asks you to change 5 or 6 habits at once. An exercise physiologist WebMD talked to warned that it can cause cognitive overload, and a doctor in the same piece suggested starting with one or two habits if all of them at once feels like too much [2]. There's no shame in that. A version you finish beats one you quit in week 2.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have to start over if you miss a day of 75 Soft?

No. Both Healthline and WebMD describe 75 Soft as having no restart, so you pick back up the next day. Since the rules aren't official, decide on this before you start so you're not tempted to change it on day 40.

Does walking count as exercise for 75 Soft?

Yes. WebMD says walking and yoga both count, so you don't need a gym. Just decide ahead of time how long a walk needs to be to count. Most versions use 30 to 45 minutes. A brisk walk is a great option on busy days or rest days.

Can I make up my own 75 Soft rules?

Sure. There's no official version, so most people tweak it anyway. The one thing I'd do is write your rules down before day 1. If you change them halfway through, it gets easy to keep shrinking them until they don't mean anything.

Is 75 Soft good for weight loss?

It's not really built for it. An exercise physiologist WebMD talked to said 75 Soft isn't the right fit if your goal is significant weight loss, because that takes a more intensive plan. It's better as a way to build a few healthy habits. If weight loss is the goal, talk to your doctor about a real plan.

What should I do after 75 Soft ends?

Keep the habits that stuck and drop the ones that didn't. Day 76 is where a lot of people slide back to where they started, so pick one or two habits to keep going on purpose. Moving every day is a good one to keep.
