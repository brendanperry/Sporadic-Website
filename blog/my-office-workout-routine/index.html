My home office workout routine: how I use Sporadic to beat desk fatigue

I end the day tired, but I have hardly moved! Sporadic gets me on my feet and helps me sleep better at night.

Brendan Perry — Owner and Software Engineer Published November 23, 2026

The Problem

When you work a desk job for long enough, eventually you get tired. And it is weird because you aren't tired physically, just mentally. So when you try to sleep at night, you feel tired, but you just can't sleep. Almost like your brain and your body aren't agreeing on what you need. 

The Solution

In my experience, the best thing to do is get your body and mind aligned. That means when you workout your brain at your job, you should also workout your body. 

For most people, myself included, sticking to a workout plan is difficult. Sporadic helps me solve this problem. Yes, I still try to do a full workout routine, but I need some exercise every day regardless of my motivation. That's where Sporadic kicks in.

My Setup

My Sporadic setup gets me up several times throughout the day, but it is also fairly minimal. The more things you add, the more likely you may be to skip. I have a stack setup with family members for doing push-ups every day. Read more about our 1,000 push-up challenge. I also do the daily community challenge every day after lunch.

The two of these ensure I do at least 30 push-ups and another random exercise. No, it isn't a lot, but you can setup Sporadic however you like. My 4 kids take up the rest of my physical energy, along with the occasional full workout session.

Conclusion

Don't let your desk get in the way of taking care of your body. Download Sporadic, and get on your feet. It is free after all, may as well give it a shot!
