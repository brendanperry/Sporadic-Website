The 1,000 push-up challenge I'm doing with my dad and brother

A 1,000 push-up challenge means doing 1,000 push-ups within a set number of days. Our goal is to each do 1,000 push-ups in one month. Here's how we set it up and how it's going.

Brendan Perry — Owner and Software Engineer Published November 23, 2026

The Background

Well, it isn't much of a story. Who doesn't wish at the end of the day that they had done a bunch of push-ups? Unfortunately, it doesn't always work out that way. My goal was to do 1,000 push-ups in the month of December. 

Family Support

The idea is nice, but everything comes down to execution. I enlisted my brother and father to hold me accountable, and so we could do 3,000 push-ups between the three of us. 

Sporadic Setup

The setup is simple. One custom stack with one push-up activity set to 30-35 reps. We get a workout reminder at 7:00 AM every morning with 24 hours to complete. This let's us complete them all at our own best time during the day.

How It Is Going

We kicked it off a week ago and are riding a 7 day streak so far! We are already over 900 push-ups total, which is awesome. Sporadic makes this super easy. We can track our streak, stats, and we get notified when another person completes their challenge. When I get a push notification that my dad did some push-ups, it motivates me to get mine done too.
