Get the app

Random challenges. Real friends. No gym.

Sporadic drops short exercise challenges into your day — and your group's — throughout the week. Pick a Stack, set how hard it goes, and race each other to finish before the clock runs out.

Download on the App Store

Free to download. Requires iPhone.

How it works

Three decisions up front, then Sporadic handles the timing.

1

Pick a Stack

Start with a ready-made Stack — Office Breaks, Wake Up Call, Daily Push-Ups, Walk Reminders — or build a custom one from the exercises you actually like.

2

Set the difficulty

Light, Medium, or Tough. Sporadic scales the reps to match, then decides when the challenges show up so you can't plan around them.

3

Move together

Challenges land for your whole group at once. Hold to complete, keep the streak going, and watch your totals climb in Insights.

A closer look

Stacks, group challenges, streaks, and stats on your Home Screen.
